The short version

  • Gigglebox doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information about you or your child.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Photos, voices, recordings, chats and activity stay on your device. We don’t run servers, so they have nowhere to go.
  • The app goes online only to play videos you approve (from YouTube) and, if you leave it on, to show hand-picked photos (from picsum.photos).

This policy covers the Gigglebox app for iPhone and iPad, made by Mohammad Faizan Atiq (“we”, “us”). Gigglebox is a pretend phone for young children, set up and managed by a parent or guardian.

What we collect

Nothing. Gigglebox has no sign-up and no login. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it doesn’t use the advertising identifier. We never receive your child’s name, photos, voice, messages or activity, and we can’t see how the app is used.

What stays on your device

Everything you and your child add is saved inside Gigglebox’s private storage on the device, which iOS keeps separate from other apps.

During a pretend call, the microphone measures how loud the room is so Gigglebox knows when your child has stopped talking. That audio goes to a temporary file that is deleted when the call ends. It isn’t kept, listened to or sent anywhere.

When Gigglebox uses the internet

Videos you approve (YouTube)

When you add a video link, Gigglebox asks YouTube for the video’s title and thumbnail. When your child watches it, the video plays through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) with YouTube’s own controls, links and suggestions turned off. Gigglebox doesn’t keep YouTube cookies or website data between videos.

Like any website, YouTube receives the request for the video, including the device’s IP address and basic device information, and handles it under Google’s privacy policy. Gigglebox doesn’t send YouTube your child’s name or anything else about your family.

Explore photos (picsum.photos)

If Explore photos is on, the Photos app downloads a set of hand-picked animal, nature and food pictures from picsum.photos. The service sees the request and the device’s IP address. You can turn this off in Parent settings, under Photos.

Nothing else

Pretend calls, Chat, Letters, Voices, Camera and the activity report all work without the internet.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, Chat uses Apple’s on-device language model to write family members’ replies. The model runs on the device itself; your child’s messages aren’t sent to us or to Apple’s servers. On other devices, Chat uses a built-in set of replies.

Permissions

  • Camera: for the Camera app and for taking a contact photo. Asked the first time it’s needed.
  • Microphone: for voice recordings, voice lines and pretend calls.
  • Add to Photos: only if you switch on saving your child’s pictures to your photo library. This permission can only add pictures; Gigglebox can’t read your library.

Choosing family photos uses Apple’s photo picker, which shares only the pictures you select. You can change any permission in the Settings app.

Children’s privacy

Gigglebox is made for young children playing with a parent’s help. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, which is consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR. Parent settings, and the only link that leaves the app (to this policy), sit behind a grown-up check. There are no ads, purchases or chat with real people inside the app.

Sharing and backups

Gigglebox shares nothing on its own. If you use the share button in Parent settings to send a picture or an activity report, it goes only where you choose to send it.

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, iOS may include Gigglebox’s data in your device backup. Backups are managed by Apple under your Apple Account, and we have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can delete recordings, family photos, camera pictures, chats and activity history in Parent settings. Deleting the Gigglebox app removes everything it stored on the device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you. We don’t hold any, so there’s nothing for us to hand over or erase, but you’re welcome to ask us anything about it.

Changes to this policy

If Gigglebox ever changes how it handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
