How do I open Parent settings?

On the Gigglebox home screen, press and hold the lock button, then answer the grown-up question.

How do I keep my child inside Gigglebox?
  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access, and turn it on.
  2. Tap Passcode Settings and set a Guided Access passcode.
  3. Open Gigglebox and triple-click the side (or Home) button, then tap Start.
  4. To finish, triple-click again and enter your passcode.
How do I add a video?

In Parent settings, open Approved videos and paste a youtube.com, youtu.be or Shorts link. Your child can only watch videos on that list: there’s no search, no suggestions and no comments.

Why won’t a video play?

Some YouTube creators don’t allow their videos to play inside other apps, and videos need an internet connection. If a video won’t add or play, try a different one.

How do family voices work on calls?

Each family member has a friendly built-in voice. In Parent settings, under Family & calls, you can also record your own greetings and replies so calls sound like the real grandma or dad. Recordings stay on the device.

Can family members speak Urdu?

Yes. Choose Urdu when you first set up Gigglebox, or change Call language in Parent settings. Urdu is in beta and covers pretend calls; Letters and app names stay in English for now.

Why does Chat say it uses Apple Intelligence?

On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, family members write their Chat replies with Apple’s on-device model, so they can answer what your child actually typed. On other devices, Chat uses a built-in set of friendly replies. Either way, nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I save a picture my child took?

Yes. In Parent settings, open Camera and tap a picture, then Save to Photos or Share. You can also switch on saving every new picture to your photo library.

How do I delete everything?

You can clear recordings, photos, camera pictures, chats and activity in Parent settings. Deleting the app removes everything Gigglebox stored. We don’t keep a copy, so there’s nothing to delete on our side. Read the privacy policy for details.
