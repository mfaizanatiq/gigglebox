You decide what’s inside

Your child only meets what you put there. There’s no search, no feed and no way to reach a stranger.

  • The people

    You add the family members, their photos and their voices. Calls and chats are pretend and never reach anyone.

  • The videos

    Only the ones you approve, with a daily time limit you set.

  • The settings

    Locked behind a press-and-hold and a grown-up question, so little fingers can’t change them.

Everything your child makes stays on the device

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t run servers, so there’s nowhere for it to go.

  • Family photos

    Copied in from your library. The rest of your library stays unseen.

  • Voices and recordings

    Family greetings and your child’s own recordings.

  • Camera pictures

    Saved to your photo library only if you switch that on.

  • Chat

    Apple Intelligence writes replies on the device itself.

  • Pretend calls

    Nobody is really called, and nothing your child says is kept.

  • Activity report

    For you only, removed automatically after 90 days.

It goes online for two things

  • Videos you approve

    They play through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, with no cookies kept between videos.

  • Explore photos

    Hand-picked animal and nature pictures from picsum.photos. You can turn it off.
